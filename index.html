09 / FAQ

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything new members and visitors may want to know about Skywolf.

What is SKYWOLF STUDIO?

SKYWOLF STUDIO is a remote fan dubbing team focused on anime voice acting, dubbing and creative collaboration.

Can new dubbing artists join?

Yes. New joiners are welcome to learn, practice and develop their dubbing skills with the team.

Do experienced artists have to join?

Experienced voice artists, editors, designers and creators are also welcome to contribute.

Is the team remote?

Yes. Skywolf is designed around remote collaboration, allowing members to work together from different locations.

What roles are available?

Dubbers, editors, designers, creators, managers and other creative roles can be part of the team.
